04Questions

Questions

01What does "Indicative · T0" mean?

Every number the studio prints is computed in your browser on one weather year with the interactive light tier, T0, and stamped "Indicative · T0" on screen and on every exported file. A finite-parcel tier, T1, is in build. A hosted ray-traced tier, T2, is planned, and it is the tier an engineered report would rest on.

02Which countries does it cover?

Indonesia first, South Africa second. The studio maps 20 jurisdictions to a currency and locale across 14 currencies. Cost rate books cover South Africa, Indonesia, Germany and the United States, with a generic book everywhere else; crop prices cover South Africa, Indonesia, the United States, Germany and Italy.

Compliance screening covers Germany, France, Italy, Japan and two US states, with voluntary profiles for South-East Asia and Southern Africa.

03Is the compliance check a permit?

No. It is screening, not legal advice. Each applicable regime is run rule by rule with the metric, threshold, margin, clause and source. Voluntary and draft rules never show a pass.

04Which weather data can a paid project use?

Three sources are wired. Open-Meteo's ERA5 feed is the default, and its free API is non-commercial. NASA POWER and PVGIS SARAH-3 both allow commercial use. The weather card shows a licence chip for each before you build.

05Is there a Bahasa Indonesia interface?

Yes. The studio header has an EN / ID toggle for the steps and the result tabs. The farmer's offer PDF prints in either language; the other exports are in English.

06What does Monitoring include?

A sensor kit generated for your design, each line tagged to the regulation or design rule it serves. Logger uploads in 8 formats, from Campbell TOA5 to ChirpStack v4. And a paired analysis of the array against a control plot: block bootstrap with AR(1) and Newey-West intervals, BACI difference-in-differences, and a Welch-log / Fieller yield ratio. Live telemetry is not yet in the studio.
