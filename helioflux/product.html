06Compare · Bandingkan

Searching the design space

Save builds side by side, sweep two parameters at once, or ask the studio what to build for a crop, or what to grow under a structure.

Saved variants
Up to 12 builds side by side, every measure as a delta against the baseline.
Explore designs
A two-parameter sweep of up to 25 full engine runs, plotted as energy against relative crop yield with the Pareto front. Apply any point.
What should I build?
For a crop: a Latin-hypercube search over every structure with a three-way Pareto front of energy, yield and CAPEX. For a structure: crops ranked by evidence-weighted yield and price; a crop with no evidence is refused.
