See your field in the studio

A walkthrough on a real parcel, yours if you have one. Bring a boundary file or just a location and a crop.

  1. Place the parcelDraw it, or import GeoJSON, KML, KMZ, a zipped Shapefile or a CSV of corners.
  2. Pick the crop and the structureFrom 35 crops and 5 archetypes, with the suitability of each shown up front.
  3. Build and read the answerEnergy, crop, land-equivalent ratio and NPV in one sentence, then the 16 tabs behind it.
  4. Leave with the filesA dossier PDF, a compliance screening PDF and an Excel workbook, all stamped "Indicative · T0".

Useful to bring

  • A field boundary, or the coordinates of the site
  • The crop you grow now, and your own open-field yield if you know it
  • Your local power price or tariff, and any lease terms you have in mind
