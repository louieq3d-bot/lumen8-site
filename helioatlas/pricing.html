02Questions

About the plans.

What does the trial include?
Every feature, on Team quotas: 5 seats and 200 designs a month.
When does billing start?
Billing opens at launch. Until then the Account drawer in the studio shows the plans and your usage.
What currency are the prices in?
US dollars, per month. The country presets inside the studio set the currency of your customer's savings and proposal, not the price of a plan.
Does my customer need a seat?
No. The customer opens the online proposal link in a browser, with no account, and signs with a name, an email and a tick. Seats are for the people who design.
How do I get access?
Request access on the start page. There is no self-serve sign-up, and the start page says exactly what happens to your request.
