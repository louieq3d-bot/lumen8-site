What this site keeps.

In plain words: what the code of this website stores in your browser, what it sends, and what it loads from elsewhere.

Updated 9 October 2026

Nothing you type into this site leaves your browser.

This page covers the code of the pages in this site's menu. It does not cover the server that delivers them, or the HelioAtlas studio itself.

The forms keep your entry here

The two forms on the start page, Request access and Book a demo, are not yet connected to an inbox, so submitting one sends nothing. The entry is kept in your browser's local storage under one key, ha.forms, which holds your 20 most recent entries. Each records the form, the time, the page and what you filled in:

  • Request access: work email, company, country and what you design.
  • Book a demo: name, work email, company, country, team size and an optional message.

Local storage stays in this browser on this device until you clear it, and other websites cannot read it.1

No cookies, no analytics

The site's own code sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking or advertising scripts. It does not ask for your location, camera or microphone. Copy details, on the start page, writes to your clipboard only when you press it.

What loads from where

Pages, styles, scripts, fonts, images and films are served from this site itself, with one exception: the 3-D scene at the top of the home page loads a 3-D graphics library from a public content-delivery network. On that page your browser requests the script from that network's servers and, as with any web request, sends your IP address and the name of your browser. The scene's terrain and imagery files come from this site.

How to clear it

Press the button below, or clear this site's stored data in your browser's settings. A private browsing session keeps nothing once you close it.
