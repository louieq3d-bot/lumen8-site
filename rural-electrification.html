Screen every unelectrified village in a country. Pick the ones to build first. Take each one from a map click to a construction pack, with the paperwork a lender expects.

01 · SCREEN

Rank the whole country.

Six signals per village: households without power, solar resource, distance from the grid, accessibility, hazard exposure and demand. One ranked list, filterable by province, tier and terrain.

02 · TWIN

Rebuild the village in 3D.

Real building footprints and heights, road extraction and terrain become a digital twin. The low-voltage network is drawn house by house and checked against code.

03 · SIZE

Size the system hour by hour.

Solar and battery are dispatched across 8,760 hours for 25 years. Yield is reported at P50 through P99, with Monte Carlo runs behind every headline.

04 · FINANCE

Match the money to the village.

Ability to pay, willingness to pay and three tariff scenarios feed a funding stack that bundles the right instruments and pre-fills the applications.

05 · BUILD

Hand the crew a pack.

Bill of quantities with sourced components, pole schedules, drawings, logistics routes and an offline field briefing for the team on the ground.

06 · VERIFY

Prove it from orbit.

Built assets are re-observed by satellite. Impact and carbon are measured against what exists, and the result is signed so it cannot be quietly edited.

Why it matters

Small sites,
big money.

A single village is too small for institutional capital and too expensive to study one at a time. Lumen8 removes both problems at once.

01

The study cost disappears

What used to be a bespoke consultant engagement per site becomes a repeatable run. Portfolios of hundreds of villages are screened in an afternoon.

02

Sites become portfolios

Because every village is analysed the same way, they can be bundled into fundable tranches with consistent risk and return figures.

03

Engineering comes with the finance

Lenders see a design, not a sketch. Drawings, bills of material and code checks sit beside the financial model in the same dossier.
