Feasibility studies

Pre-feasibility and feasibility studies for utility-scale solar and storage, mini-grids and rural electrification programmes, built on the Lumen8 engine and delivered by people who answer your questions.

How it works

  1. Scoping call (30 minutes): your sites, your decision, the inputs you have.
  2. Statement of work: scope, deliverables, dates, milestones and a fixed price, for you to sign.
  3. Study: the engine runs, an analyst checks and explains every result; you see interim results on the platform.
  4. Delivery and review: report, data files and a walk-through; corrections within the acceptance window.

Every study names its data sources and assumptions and is governed by the terms of service and the statement of work.

Questions

What does a Lumen8 feasibility study cost?

Each study is priced per site or per programme after a short scoping call, because a single 50 MW solar site and a 300-village electrification plan are different jobs. You get a fixed price and a milestone schedule in the statement of work before anything starts.

How long does a study take?

A pre-feasibility screen of one site or one district takes about two to three weeks from receiving the inputs. A full feasibility study with design, yield, cost and finance takes about four to eight weeks. The statement of work fixes the dates.

What do you need from us?

The site boundary or the area of interest (KMZ, shapefile or coordinates), what you already know about demand and the grid, any tariff and financing assumptions you must use, and a contact who can answer questions. We fill the rest from satellite, weather, grid and census data and say where each input came from.

Is the study bankable?

A Lumen8 study is a planning and feasibility study that names its data sources, assumptions and uncertainty. Lenders usually still appoint their own independent engineer for financial close; our deliverables are built so that engineer can check every number.

Who owns the results?

You do. Once paid, you may use the deliverables for any purpose of your organisation and share them with lenders, partners, contractors and authorities.

Can we use the platform ourselves?

Yes. Study customers get access to the Lumen8 platform for their study area during the study, and the agent API is available to teams that want to run the engine from their own tools.
