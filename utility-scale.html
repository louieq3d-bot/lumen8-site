Questions

What engineers
ask first.

The pipeline is built to be interrogated. These are the questions it gets most.

What can we send as a site?

A boundary as KML, GeoJSON or a shapefile, or a coordinate with a target capacity and a rough sketch of the land. Exclusion zones, setbacks and access are solved from there. Eight curated reference sites run fully offline for a first look.

Where do the component prices come from?

From a catalogue of 170 sourced component SKUs with regional cost multipliers. The bill of materials is itemised, so your own quotes can replace catalogue lines one by one and the finance stage re-runs on your numbers.

What is different about Bankable mode?

Interactive is for exploring and Standard gives a defensible design. Bankable runs the full chain with conservative defaults, records every assumption and lists every warning that was not cleared, so a lender's engineer sees exactly what was and was not done.

Can we edit the drawings and the model?

Yes. Layouts, single-line diagrams and schedules export as DXF, civil surfaces as LandXML, and the fourteen-tab workbook holds every number behind them. Nothing is locked inside the platform.
