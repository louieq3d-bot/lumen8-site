Common questions

Before you sign up

Can I sign up on my own?
Not yet. AgrosIQ is in a design-partner pilot: we set up each partner's account and fields with them, and agree pilot terms in writing. Self sign-up, the Free tier and online billing come later.
What does the pilot cost?
Pilot pricing is agreed with each partner. The tiers on the pricing page show what we plan to sell; their prices are not set yet.
How long does setup actually take?
Once your field outlines are in, the decade of history and the latest satellite pass load in about five minutes for a field of around 30 hectares. During the pilot we do the setup with you.
Is my farm data private?
Your fields and notes belong to you and we never sell them. To fetch data for a field, its location goes to the satellite-data and weather services listed in our privacy notice (available on request). Ask and we delete your fields from the live service; deletion from backups, and export, are being built.
