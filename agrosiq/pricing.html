Pricing questions

Can I sign up today?
Not on your own yet. AgrosIQ is in a design-partner pilot: we set up each partner's account and fields with them and agree pilot pricing directly. Self sign-up, the Free tier and online billing come later.
Why are there no prices?
Pilot pricing is agreed with each partner while we measure what AgrosIQ is worth on real farms. We will publish prices when self sign-up opens.
Is there a free trial?
Not yet. During the pilot we agree terms with each partner instead.
How will hectares be counted?
By the area of the fields you add, across all your farms. Roads, yards and bush you don't draw won't count.
Is there a contract?
Pilot terms are agreed in writing with each partner. Our draft terms, the starting point, are available on request.
What happens to my data if I leave?
It's yours. Ask and we delete your fields from the live service. Deletion from backups and data export are being built; until then we tell you what remains and for how long.
