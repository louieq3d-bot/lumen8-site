Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?
Yes. Up to 40 hectares, free forever, no card needed. It's the full health map on every pass, so you can see AgrosIQ working on your own fields before you spend a cent.
Can I try a paid plan first?
Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Pick a plan, add your fields and see everything it can do. If it isn't for you, you drop back to Free automatically.
How are hectares counted?
We count the area of the fields you add, across all your farms. Roads, yards and bush you don't draw aren't counted. You can add or remove fields at any time.
Can I change plans later?
Any time. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period. Your history always stays with your fields.
Is there a contract?
No lock-in. Pay monthly and cancel whenever you like, or pay yearly and save 20%.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
It's yours. Export everything with one click, and if you ask us to delete it, we delete it.
