Questions from the people who'll use it

I'm a single grower with a few paddocks. Is AgrosIQ for me?
Yes. AgrosIQ is in a design-partner pilot: we set up your fields with you and agree pilot pricing directly. A Free tier for small farms is planned for when self sign-up opens.
I'm an agronomist with lots of clients. Can I keep my own branding?
Not yet. Branding, client workspaces and invitations are coming later. Today you can see several client farms on one account and share read-only links to single fields.
I run a large operation with multiple managers. Who can see what?
Roles are coming later. During the pilot, everyone on your account sees every field on it, and read-only links share single fields with people outside it.
We're an agribusiness with a grower program. Can we use it at scale?
We are building the portfolio features with our first partners. Today you get the field record and the spots to check for every enrolled field. Talk to us about the pilot.
How is my data handled?
Your fields and records belong to you and we never sell them. Connections use HTTPS. To fetch data for a field, its location goes to the satellite-data and weather services listed in our privacy notice (available on request). Ask and we delete your fields from the live service; deletion from backups, and export, are being built.
