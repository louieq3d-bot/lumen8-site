Questions from the people who'll use it

I'm a single grower with a few paddocks. Is AgrosIQ for me?
Yes. You can watch your first 40 hectares free, forever, with every feature turned on. As your operation grows, upgrade only when you're ready — no contracts, cancel any time.
I'm an agronomist with lots of clients. Can I keep my own branding?
Yes. Farm and Plan plans let you invite your growers, run reports in your own name and share a single map with everyone you advise. You'll see your clients ranked by what needs you today, every morning.
I run a large operation with multiple managers. Who can see what?
You decide. Invite your team and set who can view which fields, who gets alerts, who can edit records and who only watches. Roles match how your operation actually runs.
We're an agribusiness with a grower program. Can we use it at scale?
Yes. AgrosIQ Enterprise rolls up thousands of paddocks into one sourcing view, with field-level outlook refreshed every pass and grower programs scored against ten seasons of history. Talk to us to set it up.
How is my data handled?
Your fields and records belong to you. We never sell your data, and you can export or delete it at any time. It's encrypted in transit and at rest, private by default, and visible only to the team you choose.
