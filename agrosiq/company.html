The manifesto

The thing

A farmer's most important work happens before anyone can see it. The way a canopy thins in late spring, the way a low spot drowns out the row before the season turns, the way one corner of a field quietly falls behind. We believe every grower should see those signals the moment they happen — instantly, in plain language, on every field they farm — so good seasons become great ones and bad ones don't have to be.

Why now

Three forces came together

For the first time in history, we can watch every farm the way we already watch the weather. Each of these shifts made AgrosIQ possible.

Space got closer

A new generation of satellites watches the planet in sharper detail, more often. What was once a rare glimpse is now a steady look at every field, every few days.

Why is the north block behind?
It's 18% below its normal. A dry patch in the NE corner worth a walk today.

AI got useful

The same models that write sentences can read a season of imagery and tell you, in three lines, what your farm is trying to say. Plain English. No jargon.

Farming got harder

Margins are tighter, weather is wilder, and the cost of getting it wrong keeps rising. The farmers who win the next decade will be the ones who see it coming first.

What we believe

Five things we won't compromise on

These aren't posters in the kitchen. They're how we decide what to build, what to ship and what to kill.

Farmers first

Every decision starts in a paddock. If it doesn't help a grower walk less, catch more or earn more, it doesn't ship. Every demo, every roadmap, every line of copy is read once with the farmer in mind.

Show the evidence

No black boxes. Every alert is tied to the pass it came from, the pixel it sits on, and the season behind it. If we can't show our work, we don't make the claim. Trust is built one map at a time.

Your data is yours

Private by default, encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, exported or deleted on your word. The fields you trust us with belong to you, not to us.

Simple beats clever

If a grower can't explain it to their neighbour over the fence, we redesign. Clever is easy; clear is hard, and hard is what we ship.

Built for every farm

A forty-hectare plot and a forty-thousand-hectare estate use the same product, on the same terms. We don't gate features behind tiers; we make sure the small farm isn't a stripped-down version of anyone else's.

How we work

Four principles, every day

No slogans on the wall — just the way we build, ship and listen.

01

Start with the field

Every feature begins in a paddock with a grower who's never seen it. We don't whiteboard until we've walked the rows and watched a real farmer try to use what we've sketched.

02

Watch every pass

If a satellite reads a field, we read it. Nothing is missed on purpose. Every alert we send is one a careful farmer would have sent themselves if they had the time.

03

Explain everything

Every answer on AgrosIQ shows its work. The maps, the dates, the season behind the call. No magic, no hand-waving, no "trust us."

04

Grow with our growers

The growers who use AgrosIQ shape the next thing we build. They write in, we read every line. A surprising amount of our roadmap is just good letters from the field.
