Questions, answered

Do I need any hardware, drones or sensors?
None at all. AgrosIQ works from space. Sign in, draw your fields and you're done. It works on your laptop, tablet and phone.
What happens when it's cloudy?
AgrosIQ keeps reading your fields straight through cloud, so they stay watched through the wettest weeks of the season.
How far back does my history go?
Every field comes with around ten years of seasons, loaded the day you add it. No waiting a year for the system to learn your farm.
Which crops does it work with?
Broadacre grains, oilseeds, pulses, cotton, sugarcane, pasture, vines and orchards. If it grows in a field and you can see it from above, AgrosIQ can watch it.
Is my farm data private?
Yes. Your fields and records belong to you. We never sell your data, and you can export or delete it at any time.
