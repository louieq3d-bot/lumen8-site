01 · Capture

A sky full of eyes for every field.

AgrosIQ listens to multiple satellites at once and reads every pass over your farm. Multi-satellite imaging, every few days. No satellite you have to book. Every pass is read; cloudy ones are marked.

02 · Clarity

A clear view, even when the sky is not.

Cloudy week? The Engine masks cloud, though some haze still slips through, and a radar estimate fills in, drawn with its error range. Every image is cleaned and aligned so each pixel means the same thing every time you look at it.

03 · Memory

A decade of seasons, loaded on day one.

Every field you add arrives with about ten years of its own archived imagery already inside. The Engine stacks the seasons and knows what a great year, a tough year, and a normal year look like — for that exact patch of ground.

04 · Insight

From pixels to one clear place to look.

Models and each field's own history turn every fresh pass into the one thing a farmer needs to know: walk here. The amber pixel on the exact 10-metre spot. What it is, and what to do, is your call.

Your data is yours

Owned by you. Built to stay that way.

Every field you add, every season that loads, every question you ask — that's your data. AgrosIQ never sells it, sends it over encrypted connections, and deletes it from the live service when you ask.

  • Services namedOur privacy notice (on request) lists every outside service that receives field locations, and why.
  • Never soldYour data is not the product. It is not sold, brokered or traded. It is yours.
  • Encrypted in transitConnections to AgrosIQ use HTTPS.
  • Export: coming laterCSV, KML and shapefile export of maps and season data is being built.
  • Deletion on requestAsk and we delete your fields from the live service. Deletion from backups is being built.
