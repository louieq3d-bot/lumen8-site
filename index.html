The pack
Why it can be trusted

Honest
by design.

A platform that decides where money goes has to show its working. Lumen8 is built so that nothing can quietly pretend to be better than it is.

01

Every number carries its source

Each dataset is stamped with where it came from and how fresh it is. Real, modelled and placeholder data are labelled differently on screen.

02

Every run is reproducible

The same inputs give the same outputs, and each run is hashed so a reviewer can confirm the document they hold matches the analysis that produced it.

03

Every design is checked against code

Electrical designs run through standards-based rule sets before they leave the platform. Failures are reported, not hidden.

04

Every claim is verifiable from orbit

Built assets are re-observed by satellite so impact and carbon are measured against what actually exists on the ground.

Questions

Before you
write to us.

The things people ask in the first call, answered plainly. Anything else, ask.

What makes it a sustainable development platform?

It covers the infrastructure sustainable development actually runs on: clean power, energy access, food, water, connectivity and trade, on one engine and one standard of evidence. Every module designs, prices and finances its asset, every design carries its carbon ledger, and every result states which goals it serves and how that was measured.

What do we need to send you to start?

A coordinate, a boundary as KML or GeoJSON, or a list of sites. For a region, a province name is enough. If you have a target capacity, an offtake in mind or a lender's requirements, send those too and the run is shaped around them.

How long does a run take?

Screening a region is a matter of minutes. A full engineering and financing case for one site runs in under an hour of compute. The time that matters is the review with you, which is a working session, not a project.

Where does the data come from?

Satellite imagery, building footprints, terrain, decades of reanalysis weather, grid maps, hazard and climate models and national statistics, from public and licensed sources. Every value on screen is stamped with its source and its date, and modelled or placeholder data is labelled as such.

Does it replace an engineer?

No. It produces what an engineer would otherwise spend weeks assembling, with code checks and a review trail, so their time goes into judgement instead of collation. The pack is built to be checked, and it says what it assumed and what it could not do.

Which countries does it cover?

The platform was built and proven on Indonesia. The utility-scale and agrivoltaics engines run anywhere on Earth with the grid code and currency chosen explicitly; wind covers Southeast Asia; hydro starts with Indonesia. Country coverage for the village, port, tower and agriculture modules is extended by request.

How do we get access?

By engagement. Tell us what you are trying to decide and we will propose a pilot on your own sites. If it earns its place, it becomes a seat on the platform for your team.
